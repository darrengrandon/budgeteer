## Goal
Off by default; on for data that already has purchases. When on: the Spending card on
Home (left to spend, "Log a purchase", latest activity) and a purchase sheet (amount,
optional note) with the overspend block. Off hides, never deletes.

## Done when
Phone tests first: default off for new teens, on after restoring a backup with purchases,
exact zero and one cent over, switching off and on keeps purchases. `./check.sh` passes.
